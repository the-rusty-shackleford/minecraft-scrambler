# D-0001 — A dirt bike that jumps as a horse does

**The ask** (Rusty, 2026-10-23): add the dirt bike model received that day to the Vanilla Wheels
roster; it jumps exactly as a ridden horse does, same controls; a cool name. The name settled with
him: the **Scrambler**. One seat.

**Decision**:
1. A data-only repo on Vanilla Wheels 1.19.0, which added what a bike needs (its D-0047): the horse's
   jump (`jump`, an average horse's 0.7), the lean (`lean`, at most 40 degrees), parts of the model that
   move (the fork steering with the front wheel in it, both wheels rolling), and a rolling part counting
   as a wheel on the lift. Nothing here is Java but the gametests and the booth.
2. **The model is adopted as received**: kept byte for byte as the reference, fitted with a profile,
   never redrawn. The only change to the game's copy is the red parts' texture made grey for the dye,
   with a factory colour that gives the received red back undyed (`tools/bbgen/paint.py`).
3. **Upright parked.** The model's kickstand is stowed along the swingarm and too short to reach the
   ground from its mount, so it is left as drawn and the bike stands upright.
4. **Its numbers**: lighter, quicker off the mark and tighter than a truck, a little slower flat out
   (0.75); six steel ingots for the chassis, six to rebuild a wreck; half a truck's tank; durability 3.
   No horn: Vanilla Wheels has only a truck's.

**Alternatives**: the horse's own strength drawn per bike, as horses are born (refused: Rusty asked
for the horse's jump, and one fixed average keeps every Scrambler the same machine); a stronger jump
for a dirt bike (left to Rusty: the profile's `strength` is one number).
